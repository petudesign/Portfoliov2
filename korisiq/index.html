KorisIQ

Making Finnish basketball data easier to explore, understand and use, starting with the Women's Korisliiga.

KorisIQ explores how performance data can become more useful when it is connected to player context, questions and decisions.

Role
Product framing, UX/UI and data-led interface direction
Context
Finnish basketball
Status
Work in progress
Work in progress
Problem

Basketball data is available, but not easy to use.

Statistics can answer more useful questions when the interface helps people move from what happened to why it happened.

My contribution

Product framing and data-led interface direction.

I am working on how statistics, player context and visual analysis can become useful product decisions.

Current state

An active product and data exploration.

The interface direction is still being tested around what coaches, players and interested viewers need to understand.

The question

How might basketball data support clearer analysis without turning the product into a wall of disconnected statistics?

My contribution

I am shaping the product framing, interaction direction and relationship between data, context and the questions a user wants to answer.

What is still open

The product still needs evidence about which users, comparisons and moments make the data genuinely useful.

From numbers to understanding

KorisIQ is being shaped around three connected questions: what happened, why it happened and what to try next. The challenge is not only to show more data, but to make the relationship between data and interpretation easier to follow.
