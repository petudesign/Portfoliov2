Service design · Retail UX

S-Hävikki

A food waste reduction concept for S Group's retail environment, designed to reduce waste, save money and make near-expiry checks less frustrating for store employees.

The service connects an employee workflow, an in-store S-Hävikki point and a customer-facing idea into one system.

Role
Product, UX and service design
Scope
Concept and prototype
Status
Concept, 2026
Concept and prototype
Problem

Near-expiry work is still harder to manage than it needs to be.

Employees need a clearer way to find, check and hand over products before they become waste.

My contribution

Service concept, interaction model and prototype direction.

I connected the employee workflow, the in-store point and the customer-facing idea into one service system.

Current state

A scoped concept with an interactive employee-tool prototype.

The next step is validating the workflow in a real store before expanding the app or physical setup.

What I explored

How near-expiry products could become easier to find, handle and choose through a connected employee tool, branded in-store point and digital extension.

What is worth preserving

The strongest part is the system thinking: the concept connects store operations and customer behaviour instead of treating food waste as only a campaign message.

What needs validation

The risky assumption is that better visibility will change behaviour enough. Placement, signage and employee workload need testing before adding features.

Problem framing

Finland has already made meaningful progress around food waste. This project does not frame the topic as an ignored crisis. The opportunity is more practical: if the category is already moving in the right direction, how could design help stores make the next step easier?

The concept looks at the service as a connected system rather than a single cabinet, campaign or app. The employee workflow has to become easier while the customer-facing experience still needs to feel clear and worthwhile.

Design question: How might a store make the right action easier at the moment when a near-expiry product needs attention?

Concept and prototype scope · Validation in a real store is the next step
