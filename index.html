About

I’m Petteri, but my friends call me Petu. I’m 28 and live in Helsinki. I studied ICT engineering, but I’ve always cared more about what we build, why it should exist and how people will experience it, so no single title has ever quite fit.

Most of my projects start from something I’ve run into myself or seen people put up with. I build them to learn, and because I’d rather show an idea working than describe it.

It started early: arranging stickers on my Habbo profile, building tiny blogs, putting together my first PC in vocational school, and wanting to be Dr. Lightman from Lie to Me long before I knew what behavioural science was. Product design was the first place those interests met. AI has made the building side exciting again, and I’d rather not choose between understanding a product and making it.

Away from the screen: basketball, live music, darts, art, and a good true crime story. I’m also red-green colourblind, a useful reminder that what looks obvious to me can look different to someone else.
