OpenSlot

Check your availability without reaching for your phone.

A small smart-glasses concept for checking calendar availability during a conversation. The idea is to show just enough information to answer a scheduling question at a glance.

View project on GitHub ↗

“Does Friday work for you?”

The planned interaction starts with a question in conversation. OpenSlot would recognise the mentioned day and automatically show its availability in the glasses. If nothing is scheduled, the display could simply say “Empty”. If there is an appointment, it could show “13:30–16:30 · Busy”, leaving the rest of the day visibly available.

The aim is a brief availability check that helps you answer while staying in the conversation.

Starting with the simulator

I do not own MemoMind glasses, so I plan to develop the idea using MemoMind's desktop simulator. The project is still at the concept and early groundwork stage. A working experience with a real calendar and automatic speech recognition has not been built yet.

A deliberately small project

The scope is checking availability. I want to explore how little information the display needs to make a scheduling decision easy, without turning it into a full calendar interface. The next step is to develop that interaction in the simulator before testing it on physical glasses.
