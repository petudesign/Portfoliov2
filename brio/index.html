Brio

Interrupt the scroll. Move. Continue intentionally.

Brio explores whether a short physical interruption can help people notice when scrolling has become automatic and choose what happens next.

Role
Product design, UX and prototype direction
Stack
React Native, MediaPipe, Supabase
Status
In development
In development
Question

How might doomscrolling be interrupted without treating social media as the enemy?

The product focuses on the moment when scrolling continues without a conscious decision to keep going.

My contribution

Product framing, interaction design and technical direction.

I shaped the intervention, the user flow and the relationship between pose tracking and the decision to continue.

Current state

A working product direction still being developed.

The concept needs further testing around timing, comfort, false positives and whether the interruption actually changes behaviour.

The problem

Scrolling can continue almost automatically. A warning or time limit does not always create a useful moment for reflection.

The decision

After a period of scrolling, Brio introduces a brief movement break before the user decides whether to return to the feed.

The stack

React NativeMediaPipeSupabase

A gentler interruption

The goal is not to shame the user or position social media as the enemy. It is to create a small physical and mental pause that gives the next decision a chance to become intentional.
