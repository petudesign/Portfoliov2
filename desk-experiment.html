01 / Main case In development · iOS first BRIO

Digital Wellbeing / Product Design

Brio

Interrupt the scroll. Move. Continue intentionally.

Problem

Doomscrolling often continues without a conscious decision to keep going.

Concept

After 20 minutes of scrolling, Brio introduces a short movement break before the user chooses whether to continue.

Principle

Social media is not the enemy. The goal is to make its use more deliberate and better regulated.
