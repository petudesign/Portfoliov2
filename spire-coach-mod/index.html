Spire Coach Mod

Learn from your own Slay the Spire decisions.

An early coaching mod for Slay the Spire 1. I am collecting gameplay data and exploring how a review of your own choices can help you improve.

View project on GitHub ↗

Learning from the runs I actually play

I enjoyed playing Slay the Spire, but often did not know whether I was making good decisions. Learning from my own runs felt more appealing than searching for a YouTube video I would both want to watch and actually learn from.

That is why I started building Spire Coach. It began as a way to help myself learn, and I hope it might eventually be useful to other players too.

Collecting evidence, asking questions

The current focus is gathering run data: card plays, choices and combat outcomes. For now, the experience is mainly about prompting reflection through questions, with only a small amount of rule-based feedback. It is too early to treat that feedback as a reliable assessment of a run.

The first implementation is limited to Ironclad. It records data locally and includes one narrow lesson about card order: applying Vulnerable before a Strike can increase its damage.

From reflection to useful feedback

The aim is to explain what could have been done better and why, using the actual situation rather than generic advice. Once I have enough data, I want to experiment with Jev as part of evaluating decisions. That is a future experiment, not a feature in the current mod.

The player makes the decisions

Spire Coach is intended to be a coach, not a hack or an autoplayer. It should help you understand your decisions and learn for the next run. Choosing cards, taking actions and playing the game remain with the player.
